Questions, answered

Frequently asked

How long does it take to get started?

Most teams are live in under 15 minutes. Drop in our SDK or connect a warehouse, and Helios auto-detects your schema and builds starter dashboards for you.

Do you support data warehouses?

Yes — native connectors for Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, and Postgres, with both push and pull syncing and full incremental updates.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and offer row-level permissions plus SSO/SAML on Growth and Enterprise plans.

Can I export or query my raw data?

Always. You get full SQL read access and a typed API. Your data belongs to you — export anytime, no lock-in.

What happens after the free trial?

You can pick any plan or stay on a generous free tier. We never delete your historical data, and we'll never bill you without a heads-up.
